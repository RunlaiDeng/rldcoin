# Ordinary carriage of empty timeout proposals

The ground regional BFT companion can offer an already Native-authenticated,
complete empty Proposal in its existing transport spare slots after a certified
view change. The child remains empty; its complete parent may contain only the
original bounded Import commands. Other commands, genesis shapes, epochs and
unsupported encodings retain ordinary carriage.

`regional_bft_timeout_hint.py` reconstructs the original fixed Ed25519 Native
signing encoding for rounds 1 through 31. A timeout certificate must contain
three or four distinct ordered configured keys for exactly the observed context
and preceding round. Every Timeout signature is checked. Each optional high
Prepare quorum retains three or four distinct ordered keys, its exact context,
round, value and all original vote signatures. Equal highest rounds cannot select
conflicting values. The selected high value must match the complete proposed
statement, and the leader and whole Proposal signature must match the requested
round. Round zero retains its original no-timeout encoding.

The filter handles a finite nonrecursive shape: at most four timeouts, each with
at most four high Prepare votes. It retains no verification or state result.
The existing complete-envelope byte bound and transport priority budget remain
in force. Unsupported, altered or incomplete inputs receive no extra priority;
this classification does not delete their retained evidence or declare them
invalid in the ledger.

The existing two first offers, ordinary recent/history traffic, four-packet exact
retry, signed routing, whole-packet capacity refusal, atomic preparation and cold
custody verification are unchanged. Scheduling offers are neither destination
receipts nor finality votes. Rust still independently authenticates every
complete envelope, executes the Native prefix and owner/value transitions, and
checks all locks, heads, roles and quorum conditions before releasing authority.

The public source tests use fresh public ground keys and synthetic no-value
statements. Their actual two-hop receipt is transport evidence only. They do not
establish Native work, import, maturity, sustained fault liveness, independent
operators or physical interstellar routes. Complete source-bound node, custody,
conservation and fault qualification remains mandatory.
