# Interstellar implementation and adoption gates

All frozen I1–I12 and S/R/A–G/N/P requirements remain mandatory. Local no-value
fixtures cannot authorize a network or replace independent or physical evidence.

- Consensus and custody: complete native evidence before body deduplication,
  pinned configured contacts, durable receipt before retransmit suppression,
  unchanged deadlines/quorums/capacities and retained unsuccessful operations.
- Value: unique imports, actual maturity, permanent source debit, authenticated
  provenance, channels and I=U+E+T across arbitrary declared region compositions.
- History: genesis-derived reconstruction, permanent consumed IDs, separately
  trusted latest heads, bounded retained evidence and refusal on missing, corrupt,
  stale or full storage. A finite local replay does not establish long-horizon
  availability or independent rollback protection.
- Independent qualification: separate implementations, controlled keys/operators,
  multi-host custody, power-loss and copied-key exercises, external safety review.
- Cryptography: authenticated suite/key/era evolution, finite verification horizons,
  delayed revocation, downgrade refusal and reauthentication of original evidence.
- Adoption: independently reviewed exact rules and a fresh zero-allocation signed
  genesis that excludes test assets and public fixture keys.
- Physical routes: actual causal delay, contact windows, bandwidth, failure,
  custody and lifetime evidence for each radio/laser/carrier route.
