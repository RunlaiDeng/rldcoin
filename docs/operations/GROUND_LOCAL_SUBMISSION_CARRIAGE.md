# Local submission carriage in the ground companion

The complete-origin BFT companion without a joint-epoch controller may schedule one retained local `Submission` toward the configured leader for its current Native-observed parent and round. Complete Native envelope authentication, dependency synchronization and durable retention must already have succeeded. This scheduling filter grants no command validity, custody, ledger, finality or signing authority; the destination independently performs its normal complete Native checks.

The filter requires the immutable complete-origin envelope in the exact currency and region, its original content digest and bounded complete payload, a local-retention flag, no certified value, and the existing one-to-four submission command bound. The four distinct configured validator identities determine the destination from the current parent height and round. Missing or incompatible observations, roles, profiles or local bytes provide no preference. Epoch submissions and remotely retained submissions keep ordinary service. No command, expiry, original clock, owner reservation or evidence is rewritten.

One selected history place may carry this pending recipient pair only when at least two history places are present. The last ordinary history selection and its existing frontier remain unchanged, so ordinary history continues to advance. Current consensus places and same-peer Proposal/Prepare/Commit dependencies keep their original selection. A fresh three-recipient Proposal or Prepare closure with only one history place keeps that place. The total batch remains at most four, with no extra worker or socket and no transport capacity change.

Only the original complete durable enqueue removes the pair from the waiting recipient inventory. Publication failure, cold restart or missing observations retain the ordinary complete path. Transport acknowledgments neither suppress Native authentication nor imply inclusion or mature onward spendability. Quorum, caller heads, signer locks, maturity, incident handling, cryptographic and archive bounds remain mandatory. This ground scheduling interface does not qualify sustained faults, payment percentiles, independent custody, physical routes or an adopted network upgrade.
