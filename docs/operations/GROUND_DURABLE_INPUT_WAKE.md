# Durable input scheduling for the ground companion

The complete-origin BFT ground companion without a joint-epoch controller may wake its ordinary main loop when a TCP mesh operation retains a new active packet. This schedules another complete service unit; it does not authorize an envelope, vote, ledger update, import or payment. Native authentication, current caller heads, signer locks and complete evidence checks remain required on that unit.

The TCP operation compares only bounded active packet IDs from its opened, authenticated mesh state. The notification follows successful body completion, original storage close and local lease release. Handler input, deferred authenticated input and verified outbound replies use the same mesh operation boundary. Duplicate active packets, receipt-only updates and advertisements do not request another unit. A failure in the body, write or close produces no notification. If complete evidence is already durable despite a later refusal, ordinary polling remains available; the notification grants no acknowledgment and removes no packet.

One process-local condition retains one coalesced pending bit. No packet, decoded ledger, key, authorization or saved freshness witness is retained by it. A notice during a service unit waits until that unit finishes. Every subsequent unit still waits at least the existing 0.1-second minimum idle interval; repeated notices do not reset that interval or any Native consensus, expiry or maturity clock. With no notice, the configured ordinary polling interval remains the fallback. The configured minimum interval offers no earlier intake through this mechanism. No additional service, signing, TCP or input worker is started, and all original per-unit selection and wire/state bounds remain in force.

Before an initial proposal's original due, its bounded Service wait may observe the same owner's pending notice and finish the current unit through an explicit scheduling result. That result releases no proposal, vote or timeout, even if the clock reaches due during the return. It performs no additional input selection or Native reception in the wait. The pending bit remains for the ordinary main-loop wait and its minimum idle; the next complete unit independently reopens Native and current caller heads. The original parent/round clock is retained. At or after the original due, the existing single late-intake opportunity remains bounded by that unit's original quota and frame-inspection limit. Missing, stopped, closed or foreign wait owners leave the original bounded wait in place.

An optional callback failure is counted in the TCP observation and leaves custody and periodic processing intact. A stop signal interrupts the wait; close unregisters the single callback. A callback captured before close cannot restart the stopped waiter. Other companion profiles retain their ordinary polling behavior.

The source-only checks use fresh private loopback TLS stores and preserve refusal residue. Transport receipts remain separate from Native acceptance and spendability. This scheduling interface does not qualify payment percentiles, sustained faults, independent custody, physical routes or an adopted network upgrade.
