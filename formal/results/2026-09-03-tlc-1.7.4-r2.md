## R2 value-governance model

The bounded state machine now contains all four `VALUE_CAP_0..3` levels. One
level may be proposed at a time; activation requires:

- a pending target equal to current cap plus one;
- a content-hash binding to the modeled `MainnetSafetyCase`;
- both modeled independent authorization groups (`2-of-2`);
- a one-height logical challenge period;
- activation strictly before safety-case expiry height 4;
- healthy risk state with new-value acceptance not frozen.

Frontend, administrator, and legacy-client direct upgrade attempts only record
a blocked attempt. An atomic risk-deterioration event cancels pending upgrades,
sets cap 0, invalidates the safety case, freezes new acceptance, preserves the
accepted exposure vector, and leaves every supply bucket unchanged.

Runtime evidence freshness is independent of upgrade freshness: even after a
cap was validly activated, `AcceptValue` requires both a valid safety case and
`logicalHeight < SafetyCaseExpiresAt`. Reaching expiry therefore blocks every
new local, cross-Zone, and DSC acceptance without reducing prior exposure.

## Implementation-map findings and limits

The initial implementation findings remain open: imported and rejected transit
IDs use separate Rust sets; no destination fee Nullifier or
`TransferEdgeRecord` anchor was found; and the model is not refinement-linked
to every value-bearing or Crypto Era implementation path.

R2 still abstracts one safety-case hash, one fixed expiry, two equal abstract
authorization groups, one-height challenge delay, one UMCO, one fee entitlement,
three one-unit risk paths, and an atomic risk event. It does not prove evidence
renewal/revocation, real weighted governance, monitoring latency, concurrent
requests, crash persistence, per-account/Zone/global limits, signature/QC
cryptography, deterministic encoding, proof validity/data availability,
`u128/u256` arithmetic, dynamic BFT, liveness, or implementation correctness.
